About Student Hub

Our mission

Student Hub brings together course information, attendance, results, notices and events in one accessible portal for students, faculty and administrators of CHARUSAT.

Who uses this portal

Students

View courses, attendance, results and campus notices.

Faculty

Post notices, record attendance and publish results.

Administrators

Manage user accounts, courses and site-wide content.
