Frequently asked questions

Account

How do I reset my password?

Go to the Login page and select "Forgot password" to receive a reset link by email.

Can faculty and students share one account?

No, each person must register separately with the correct role.

Academics

Where can I check my attendance?

Attendance is available on your Dashboard once you log in.

When are results published?

Results are published on the Dashboard shortly after evaluation is complete.
